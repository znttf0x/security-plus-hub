# Security+ Hub

[Português](./README.md) | **English**

A free, browser-based study platform for the **CompTIA Security+ SY0-701** exam. Everything runs
in your browser — no account, no install — and your progress is saved locally on your own device.
Study online or offline.

> All study content (questions, concepts, acronyms, and the interface) is in **Brazilian
> Portuguese**, aimed at candidates preparing for the SY0-701 exam.

## What's inside

The platform is organized into modules:

- **Questões** (Questions) — a bank of **830 commented practice questions** with an *adaptive*
  engine that prioritizes the topics you keep getting wrong. Answer options are shuffled, every
  answer reveals an explanation, and you can filter and track your progress per domain. There's an
  honest *"Não sei"* (I don't know) option and full keyboard navigation.
- **Conceitos** (Concepts) — **568 flashcards** in *scenario mode*: the front gives you a real-world
  situation and you name the concept behind it, instead of just memorizing a definition.
- **Siglas** (Acronyms) — **336 acronym flashcards** with a **1–5 confidence rating**: the cards you
  rate low come back more often. A built-in **A–Z lookup** with search lets you use it as a quick
  reference too. Acronyms with more than one official meaning (like MAC or PAM) become one card per
  meaning.
- **Simulado** (Mock exam) — a **timed mock exam** in the real format: up to **90 questions in 90
  minutes**, scored on the official 100–900 scale, with a per-domain breakdown and a *"what to
  study"* list at the end pointing to the exact lessons tied to the questions you missed.
- **Metodologia** (Methodology) — how the study material was built and reviewed.

### Go deeper on any item

Every question, concept, and acronym links out to the specific **Professor Messer** lesson (website
+ YouTube) that covers the topic. Items without a video lesson link to the matching **NIST CSRC
glossary** entry, and each card also has a **Google** search button tuned to return Portuguese
results — so you can always dig into the *why* behind an answer.

## The exam it targets — CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)

The five official domains and their weights on the exam:

| Domain | Area | Weight |
|:------:|------|:------:|
| 1.0 | General Security Concepts | 12% |
| 2.0 | Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations | 22% |
| 3.0 | Security Architecture | 18% |
| 4.0 | Security Operations | 28% |
| 5.0 | Security Program Management and Oversight | 20% |

- Exam code **SY0-701** — up to **90 questions**, **90 minutes**.
- Passing score **750** on a **100–900** scale.
- Question types: multiple choice and performance-based questions (PBQ).
- Released **November 7, 2023**.

The practice bank is distributed across these domains, and the mock exam draws a weight-balanced
set of questions so it mirrors the real exam's proportions.

## Your progress & privacy

- Your progress lives in **your browser only** — nothing is uploaded anywhere and no account is
  required.
- The app works **offline** after the first load.
- Each module lets you **export and import** a backup of your progress, so you can move it between
  browsers or devices.
